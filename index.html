Import from Instagram

Instagram doesn't let this app sync itself — export your saved posts, then drop the file(s) below to add anything new.

Open Instagram's export page
  1. Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information
  2. Select Saved (tick both "Posts" and "Collections"), format HTML
  3. Instagram emails a download link, usually within minutes to a few hours
  4. Unzip it, then drop saved_posts.html below (and saved_collections.html, optional)
